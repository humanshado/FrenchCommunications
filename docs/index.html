Communication progressive du français

An interactive course built on the 40 communication chapters of Communication progressive du français, niveau débutant (CLE International). Each chapter is one acte de parole (asking for something, asking the price, booking, complaining…) practised in real situations.

Every chapter has illustrated dialogues with audio, a shadow mode and a role mode, a picture dictionary and flashcards, grammar infographics with builders, the manières de dire, pronunciation, listening and dictation, book-style activities and role-plays. Each rubrique ends with a test.
